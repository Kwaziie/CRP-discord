CHAIN REACTION PEPTIDES
DISCORD APPLICATION PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chain Reaction Peptides ("CRP") Discord application and verification system may process information when you interact with the CRP Discord community.

1. INFORMATION PROCESSED

Depending on the verification and moderation features being used, the Service may process information made available through Discord, including:

• Discord User ID
• Discord username and display name
• Discord server membership
• Account creation date or account age
• Server join date
• Roles and verification status
• Verification attempts and results
• Basic moderation and security information

2. PURPOSE

This information may be used to:

• Verify community members.
• Provide access to protected Discord channels.
• Prevent bots, spam, scams, and abuse.
• Detect suspicious verification activity.
• Maintain verification status.
• Moderate and secure the CRP Discord community.
• Restore appropriate access for returning verified members where supported.

3. INFORMATION WE DO NOT INTEND TO COLLECT

The CRP verification system is not intended to collect:

• Discord passwords
• Discord authentication tokens
• Two-factor authentication codes
• Email passwords
• Credit or debit card information
• Cryptocurrency seed phrases or private keys

Never provide these credentials to anyone claiming they are required for CRP Discord verification.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

CRP may use Discord and third-party verification, hosting, security, or bot-management services.

Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. DATA RETENTION

Verification and security information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and protect the CRP Discord community.

Information may be deleted when it is no longer reasonably required, subject to technical, security, and legal requirements.

6. DATA SHARING

CRP does not intend to sell Discord verification information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the verification system or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Service.

7. SECURITY

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the Service. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. USER REQUESTS

Users may contact CRP staff through the official CRP Discord server with questions concerning information associated with their verification.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The "Last Updated" date will identify the latest revision.

10. CONTACT

Privacy questions can be directed to CRP staff through the official Chain Reaction Peptides Discord server.
